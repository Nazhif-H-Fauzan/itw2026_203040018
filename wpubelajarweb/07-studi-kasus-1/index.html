About WPU StreamFest

WPU StreamFest 2026 is a three-day music festival that turns Jakarta into a glowing playground of light, sound, and color. Built around the theme "Neon Nights", the festival brings together six of Indonesia's most loved artists across three stages: the Main Stage for headline performances, the Side Stage for intimate sets, and the Chill Zone for laid-back acoustic moments.

Designed for Gen Z, StreamFest is more than a concert. It is a space to sing along, discover new favorites, and share the moment with friends. The festival is eco-friendly, with reusable cups, recycling stations, and zero single-use plastic. Every transaction on site is cashless through a festival wallet, and food courts, merchandise booths, and photo spots are ready to keep the vibes going from sunset until late night.
